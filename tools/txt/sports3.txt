--dialogue
M|Welcome to basketball practice. Look at the court, everyone.|歡迎來到籃球練習。大家看看球場。
B|Coach, what does the scoreboard show?|教練，計分板顯示什麼？
M|It shows the score and the time. The referee watches the game.|它顯示比數和時間。裁判負責看比賽。
B|What are the lines on the court?|球場上這些線是什麼？
M|The sideline and the baseline are the boundaries. The centerline is in the middle.|邊線和底線是界線，中線在中間。
B|Where do we shoot a free throw?|我們在哪裡罰球？
M|At the free throw line. The foul line is a different mark behind it. The key is the painted area.|在罰球線。犯規線是它後面另一條標記。禁區是塗色的區域。
B|From the three-point line, we get three points, right?|從三分線投進可以得三分，對嗎？
M|Yes. The ball can hit the backboard, the rim, and then go through the net.|對。球可以打到籃板、籃框，然後穿過籃網。
B|What are the positions? Guard, forward, and center?|有哪些位置？後衛、前鋒和中鋒嗎？
M|Right. The guard likes to dribble and pass. The center stays close to the basket.|對。後衛喜歡運球和傳球，中鋒待在籃框附近。
B|And the bench players sit by the sideline, ready to play.|而板凳球員坐在邊線旁，隨時準備上場。
