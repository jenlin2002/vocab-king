B|Paula, Pete, today we observe small creatures in the woods.|寶拉、彼得，今天我們在樹林裡觀察小動物。
M|Look, Ms. Jane! A butterfly and a dragonfly!|你看，珍老師！有蝴蝶和蜻蜓！
B|Beautiful. Under the leaf is a ladybug and a snail.|好美。葉子下面有瓢蟲和蝸牛。
M|I hear a cicada and a cricket singing.|我聽到蟬和蟋蟀在叫。
B|On the ground, an ant is carrying food, and an earthworm is in the soil.|地上有螞蟻在搬食物，土裡有蚯蚓。
M|Oh no, a mosquito! And a spider is making a web.|糟了，有蚊子！還有一隻蜘蛛在結網。
B|Spiders catch flies and moths. A praying mantis catches grasshoppers.|蜘蛛會捉蒼蠅和蛾。螳螂會捉蚱蜢。
M|At the pond, I see a tadpole and a frog. Is that a lizard on the tree?|池塘邊我看到蝌蚪和青蛙。樹上那隻是蜥蜴嗎？
B|Yes. At night, fireflies glow. A rhinoceros beetle and a stag beetle are famous in Asia.|對。晚上螢火蟲會發光。獨角仙和鍬形蟲在亞洲很有名。
M|A bee is visiting the flowers. Is a silkworm a worm?|蜜蜂在訪花。蠶是蟲嗎？
B|Yes, it makes silk. Be careful of the scorpion, the centipede, and the flea, and a crocodile is not here!|是，牠會吐絲。要小心蠍子、蜈蚣和跳蚤，不過這裡沒有鱷魚！
M|Ha-ha, and a cockroach and a dung beetle are also insects. Nature is fun!|哈哈，蟑螂和蜣螂也是昆蟲。大自然真有趣！
