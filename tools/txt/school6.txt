B|Class, today we study shapes. Amy, what shape is this?|同學們，今天我們學形狀。艾咪，這是什麼形狀？
M|It's a triangle. It has three sides.|是三角形，有三個邊。
B|Good. And what is this one with four equal sides?|很好。那這個有四個一樣長的邊的呢？
M|A square! And a rectangle has two long sides and two short sides.|正方形！長方形有兩個長邊和兩個短邊。
B|Right. A circle is round, and an oval is like an egg.|對。圓形是圓的，橢圓形像一顆蛋。
M|What about a diamond, a parallelogram, and a trapezoid?|那菱形、平行四邊形和梯形呢？
B|They all have four sides. A polygon has many sides, and a sector looks like a pizza slice.|它們都有四個邊。多邊形有很多邊，扇形像一片披薩。
M|An ice cream cone is a cone, right?|冰淇淋甜筒是圓錐體對嗎？
B|Yes. Now the signs. Plus and minus are for adding and taking away.|對。現在來看符號。加號和減號是用來加和減。
M|And the multiplication sign and the division sign are for times and divide.|乘號和除號是用來乘和除。
B|Good. Use the greater than sign and the less than sign to compare numbers.|很好。用大於和小於符號來比較數字。
M|Then the equal sign shows the answer, and the square root symbol is for roots!|然後等號表示答案，根號用來開根號！
