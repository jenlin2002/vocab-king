M|Excuse me, officer. I lost my wallet. I want to report it.|不好意思，警官，我的皮夾不見了，我想報案。
B|Sit down, please. When and where did you lose it?|請坐。你是什麼時候、在哪裡弄丟的？
M|I think a thief took it on the bus. I should have called the police sooner.|我想是小偷在公車上偷走的，我應該早點報警。
B|Don't worry. A plainclothes officer and a patrol officer will check the bus.|別擔心，便衣警察和巡邏警察會去檢查那輛公車。
M|I saw a traffic officer outside, and a police dog, too.|我在外面看到交通警察，也看到一隻警犬。
B|Yes, our precinct has many officers. This is our police department.|對，我們分局有很多警察。這是我們的警察局。
M|Your badge and patch look very neat. What is on your duty belt?|你的警徽和臂章看起來很整齊。你的勤務腰帶上有什麼？
B|A nightstick, handcuffs, and a whistle.|警棍、手銬和哨子。
M|Do you ride the police motorcycle?|你騎警用機車嗎？
B|Sometimes. Today I'll go in the patrol car with the police hat.|有時候。今天我會戴著警帽坐巡邏車去。
M|Thank you for your help, officer.|謝謝你的幫忙，警官。
B|Of course. If you need us, just call the police.|不客氣，如果需要我們，就報警。
