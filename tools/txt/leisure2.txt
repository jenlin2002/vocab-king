M|Jack, the school band is looking for new members. Do you play any instrument?|傑克，學校樂團在徵求新團員，你會任何樂器嗎？
B|I play the piano, but I also want to try the saxophone.|我會彈鋼琴，但也想試試薩克斯風。
M|Great. We already have a trumpet, a trombone, and a tuba, so brass is full.|太好了。我們已經有小號、伸縮喇叭和低音大號，銅管樂器夠了。
B|What about woodwinds? Do you have a flute, a clarinet, or an oboe?|那木管呢？你們有長笛、單簧管或雙簧管嗎？
M|Yes. Mary plays the flute, and Tom plays the clarinet.|有，瑪麗吹長笛，湯姆吹單簧管。
B|Do you have string instruments? I love the violin and the cello.|你們有弦樂器嗎？我愛小提琴和大提琴。
M|We have those, plus a harp, and a Chinese violin for traditional songs.|有，另外還有豎琴，和演奏傳統歌曲的二胡。
B|Wow! What about a French horn and an accordion?|哇！那法國號和手風琴呢？
M|Not yet, but you can bring an acoustic or electric guitar for the pop songs.|還沒有，不過你可以帶民謠或電吉他來演奏流行歌曲。
B|I can also play the harmonica, the drum, and the xylophone a bit.|我也會一點口琴、鼓和木琴。
M|Fantastic! Even a tambourine will be useful.|太棒了！連鈴鼓也派得上用場。
B|Okay, I'll join the band. When is the first practice?|好，我加入樂團。第一次練習是什麼時候？
