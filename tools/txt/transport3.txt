--dialogue
B|Leslie, you're driving now. Do you see that sign?|萊絲莉，你現在在開車。你看到那個標誌了嗎？
M|It shows a man crossing the road. A pedestrian crossing is ahead.|上面有人在過馬路，前面有行人穿越道。
B|Yes, slow down. Now I want to turn left here.|對，減速。現在我想在這裡左轉。
M|You can't. There's a no left turn sign, and it is a one-way road.|你不能，那裡有禁止左轉的標誌，而且是單行道。
B|And a do not enter sign. Okay, let's turn right.|還有一個禁止進入的標誌。好，我們右轉。
M|Wait, what does this sign mean?|等等，這個標誌是什麼意思？
B|It means merge. You have to get in line with the other cars.|它的意思是匯入，你要跟其他車一起排進車流。
M|There is construction ahead, and then a traffic circle.|前面在施工，然後有個圓環。
B|Be careful. A hill is coming, and the sign for bumps, too.|小心，前面有陡坡，也有路面顛簸的標誌。
M|In the mountains I should watch for falling rocks.|到山區我要注意落石。
B|And a no U-turn sign means don't turn around. You learn quickly!|禁止迴轉的標誌表示不能掉頭。你學得很快！
M|I'll remember all of them before the next lesson.|下次上課前我會把它們全記下來。
