B|Today we are at the aquarium to learn about sea creatures.|今天我們在水族館認識海洋生物。
M|Look, Ms. Jane! A dolphin and a whale!|你看，珍老師！有海豚和鯨魚！
B|Whales and dolphins are sea mammals, not fish. A shark is a fish.|鯨魚和海豚是海洋哺乳類，不是魚。鯊魚是魚。
M|What is that blowfish doing?|那隻河豚在做什麼？

M|An octopus has eight arms and a squid has ten, right?|章魚有八隻腳，魷魚有十隻，對吧？

M|A jellyfish looks beautiful, but it can sting.|水母看起來很美，但會螫人。
B|A sea turtle and a seal also live here. The coral and seaweed are their homes.|海龜和海豹也住在這裡，珊瑚和海藻是牠們的家。
