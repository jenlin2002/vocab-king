B|Hi, Mia! You're new here, right? I'm Ben. Let me show you around.|嗨，米雅！你是新來的對吧？我是班，我帶你參觀一下。
M|Thanks, Ben! This school looks really big.|謝謝你，班！這所學校看起來好大。
B|We're at the school gate now. The office is just inside, and the principal's office is next to it.|我們現在在校門口。辦公室就在裡面，校長室在它旁邊。
M|What is that big building over there?|那邊那棟大建築是什麼？
B|That's the auditorium. Next to it is the cafeteria, and lunch is at noon.|那是大禮堂，旁邊是學生餐廳，中午吃午餐。
M|Great. Where can I put my books?|太好了。我的書可以放哪裡？
B|You get a locker in the hallway. The bulletin board next to it shows the class news.|你會分到走廊上的一個置物櫃，旁邊的布告欄有班級消息。
M|Where do we play sports?|我們在哪裡運動？
B|On the field, the track, or the basketball court. There is also a fountain in the school yard.|在運動場、跑道或籃球場。校園裡還有一座噴水池。
M|And if I feel sick?|如果我不舒服呢？
B|Go to the nurse's office. The library, the language lab, and the chemistry lab are all upstairs.|去保健室。圖書館、語言教室和化學實驗室都在樓上。
M|Thanks, Ben. I think I'll like this school!|謝謝你，班。我想我會喜歡這所學校的！
