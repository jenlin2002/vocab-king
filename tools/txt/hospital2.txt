--dialogue
B|Mom, I don't feel well. I think I have a fever.|媽媽，我不太舒服，我想我發燒了。
M|Let me check. Yes, you do. Do you have a headache, too?|讓我看看。對，你有發燒。你也頭痛嗎？
B|Yes, and I feel dizzy. I also have a cough.|有，而且我覺得頭暈，也有咳嗽。
M|It looks like a cold. Does your stomach hurt?|看起來是感冒。你肚子會痛嗎？
B|No, I don't have a stomachache.|不會，我沒有肚子痛。
M|Okay, let's go to the doctor now.|好，我們現在去看醫生。
B|Look, a man is coming out in a wheelchair!|你看，有個人坐著輪椅出來！
M|He broke his leg, so he needs a crutch too.|他的腿斷了，所以也需要拐杖。
B|And an old lady is using a walker.|還有一位老太太在用助行器。
M|Yes, many patients need help to walk.|對，很多病人需要幫助才能走路。
B|What is that bed with wheels?|那張有輪子的床是什麼？
M|That's a stretcher. Come on, it's our turn to see the doctor.|那是擔架。來，輪到我們看醫生了。
