--dialogue
M|Jack, can you help me set the table? Guests are coming for dinner.|傑克，你可以幫我擺餐桌嗎？客人要來吃晚餐。
B|Sure, Mom. First, a plate for each person?|好，媽媽。先每人一個盤子嗎？
M|Yes. Put the dinner knife on the right and a fork on the left.|對。餐刀放右邊，叉子放左邊。
B|Where does the salad fork go?|沙拉叉放哪裡？
M|Outside the dinner fork. And the steak knife goes next to the dinner knife.|放在餐叉外側。牛排刀放在餐刀旁邊。
B|What about spoons? I see a teaspoon and a soup spoon.|湯匙呢？我看到茶匙和湯匙。
M|The soup spoon goes on the right. The teaspoon goes beside the saucer for tea.|湯匙放右邊。茶匙放在茶碟旁邊配茶用。
B|Now the glasses. A water glass and a wineglass for each person?|現在是杯子。每人一個水杯和一個酒杯嗎？
M|Yes, but no wine for the kids. A butter knife goes on the bread plate.|是的，不過小朋友不喝酒。奶油刀放在麵包盤上。
B|And a bowl for the soup. Do we need chopsticks as well?|還有喝湯的碗。我們也需要筷子嗎？
M|Yes, for the Chinese dishes. Put the big platter in the center, with a candle and a candlestick.|需要，吃中式菜用。把大盤子放在中間，旁邊放蠟燭和燭台。
B|It looks so elegant. Everything is ready!|看起來好優雅，一切就緒了！
