M|Leslie, I'm so happy it's Friday. It was a long week.|萊斯莉，我好高興今天是星期五，這週好漫長。
B|You look energetic. Usually you are tired on Friday nights.|你看起來很有精神。你星期五晚上通常很累。
M|Tomorrow is Saturday. I'm so excited. I can't stop smiling!|明天是星期六，我好興奮，笑得停不下來！
B|But look at Paul. He is angry. Why the frown, Paul?|不過看保羅，他在生氣。保羅，為什麼皺眉？
M|He could cry! He hates his job.|他快哭了！他討厭他的工作。
B|I'm surprised. I thought you liked your job. What happened?|我很驚訝，我以為你喜歡你的工作。發生了什麼事？
M|I was embarrassed this morning. A new client came, and I was too shy to talk.|今天早上我很尷尬，有位新客戶來，我太害羞了不敢說話。
B|You even turned red. Don't worry. Smile and laugh about it!|你還臉紅了。別擔心，笑一笑，一笑置之吧！
M|This weekend, let's go to the beach. We can walk, jump, and stretch.|這個週末我們去海邊吧，可以走路、跳躍和伸展。
B|We can lie on our backs or on our stomachs, squat to pick up shells, and sit and talk.|我們可以仰躺或趴著，蹲下撿貝殼，坐下聊天。
M|And some people there do handstands, or kick a ball, or kneel to build sand castles.|那裡有些人倒立、踢球，或跪著堆沙堡。
B|Be careful not to fall flat on your back. Now, let's have our coffee.|小心別仰面摔倒。現在，我們喝咖啡吧。
