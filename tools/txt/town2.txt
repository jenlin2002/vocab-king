B|Kim, how do I mail a letter in Bigburg?|金，在大堡市要怎麼寄信？
M|Put it in an envelope and write the recipient's address on the front.|把它裝進信封，在正面寫上收件人地址。
B|Where do I put my return address?|寄件人地址要寫在哪裡？
M|In the top left corner. Don't forget the zip code.|寫在左上角，別忘了郵遞區號。
B|Do I need a stamp?|我需要郵票嗎？
M|Yes, buy a stamp at the post office. Or drop the letter in the mail drop, and the mailman picks it up.|要，在郵局買郵票。或是投進郵筒，郵差會來收。
B|The postmark shows the date, right?|郵戳上會顯示日期對嗎？
M|Right. If it's important, send it by registered mail.|對。如果很重要，就用掛號寄。
B|I want to send a package to my parents. What is the fastest way?|我想寄包裹給我爸媽，最快的方式是什麼？
M|Express mail or air mail. Sea mail is cheaper, but it takes weeks.|快捷郵件或空運。海運比較便宜，但要好幾週。
B|I'd also like to send a postcard.|我也想寄一張明信片。
M|Great. The post office is a very useful place!|太好了。郵局是個很實用的地方！
