--dialogue
B|Class, today we are at the zoo. Look, there is an elephant, a lion, and a tiger!|同學們，今天我們在動物園。看，有大象、獅子和老虎！
M|Ms. Jane, the giraffe has a very long neck.|珍老師，長頸鹿的脖子好長。
B|Yes, and the zebra next to it has black and white stripes.|對，旁邊的斑馬有黑白條紋。
M|What is that animal with a bag on its belly?|那隻肚子上有口袋的動物是什麼？
B|It's a kangaroo. The koala is from Australia, too.|是袋鼠。無尾熊也來自澳洲。
M|I love the giant panda. It eats bamboo, right?|我好喜歡大貓熊，牠吃竹子對吧？
B|Right. A polar bear lives in the cold, and a camel lives in the desert.|對。北極熊住在寒冷的地方，駱駝住在沙漠。
M|There is a monkey and a squirrel up in the tree.|樹上有猴子和松鼠。
B|Good eyes! On the farm, you find a cow, a pig, a sheep, a goat, and a donkey.|你眼力真好！在農場你會看到乳牛、豬、綿羊、山羊和驢子。
M|At home, I have a dog, a cat, and a rabbit. My brother keeps a mouse.|我在家有狗、貓和兔子，我哥哥養一隻老鼠。
B|A fox, a wolf, and a deer live in the forest. A bat sleeps in the cave.|狐狸、狼和鹿住在森林，蝙蝠睡在洞穴裡。
M|And a hippopotamus, a rhinoceros, a horse, and a bear. I love animals!|還有河馬、犀牛、馬和熊。我愛動物！
