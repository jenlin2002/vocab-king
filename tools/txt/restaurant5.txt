M|Jack, let's make dinner together. First, peel and slice the potatoes.|傑克，我們一起做晚餐。先把馬鈴薯削皮和切片。
B|Okay, Mom. Should I chop the onion, too?|好，媽媽。我也要把洋蔥切碎嗎？
M|Yes, and grate the cheese. I'll boil the water for the pasta.|要，再把起司磨碎。我來煮義大利麵的水。
B|What about the chicken? Do we marinate it?|雞肉呢？要醃嗎？
M|Yes, I marinated it yesterday. Now we can bake it in the oven or grill it.|是的，我昨天就醃好了。現在可以放進烤箱烤或燒烤。
B|I like fried food. Can we deep fry the shrimp?|我喜歡炸的東西。我們可以炸蝦子嗎？
M|Let's steam the fish and stir-fry the vegetables. Blanch the broccoli first.|我們蒸魚、炒蔬菜吧。先把花椰菜汆燙。
B|I'll crack the eggs and fry them. Listen, they are sizzling!|我來打蛋再煎。聽，滋滋作響！
M|Great. I'll simmer the soup and stew the beef for tomorrow.|太好了。我來慢火煮湯，也燉明天要吃的牛肉。
B|I can also wrap dumplings and toss the salad.|我也可以包水餃和拌沙拉。
M|Sprinkle a little salt and pepper. We can barbecue on the weekend.|撒一點鹽和胡椒。週末我們可以烤肉。
B|Cooking is fun. Maybe I'll grind some coffee for dessert.|烹飪好有趣。也許我可以磨些咖啡當飯後飲料。
