--dialogue
B|Mom, why are there so many bottles in the kitchen?|媽媽，為什麼廚房裡有這麼多瓶子？
M|These are seasonings, Jack. Salt and pepper are the most basic.|這些是調味料，傑克。鹽和胡椒是最基本的。
B|What's this brown bottle? Is it soy sauce or vinegar?|這個棕色的瓶子是什麼？是醬油還是醋？
M|That's soy sauce. The vinegar is in the clear bottle. It's sour.|那是醬油。醋在透明的瓶子裡，它是酸的。
B|And this yellow one is mustard, right? I put it on my hot dog.|這個黃色的是芥末醬，對嗎？我把它擠在熱狗上。
M|Yes. Ketchup is for french fries. For stew, I use rock candy or brown sugar.|對。番茄醬用來配薯條。燉菜時我用冰糖或紅糖。
B|What do you use for soup?|你湯裡用什麼？
M|Dried bonito for the flavor, and a little MSG, if needed.|柴魚片增加風味，需要的話加一點點味精。
B|What about oil? I see two bottles.|油呢？我看到兩瓶。
M|One is cooking oil, and the other is sesame oil. Rice wine is used with fish and meat.|一瓶是食用油，另一瓶是麻油。米酒用來處理魚和肉。
B|And curry? It smells strong.|咖哩呢？味道很濃。
M|Curry is great with rice. Spread a little butter on bread, too.|咖哩配飯很棒。麵包上也可以塗一點奶油。
