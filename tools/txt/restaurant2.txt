M|Good evening. Welcome. Do you have a reservation?|晚安，歡迎光臨。你們有預約嗎？
B|Yes, a table for two under Kim.|有，兩人桌，名字是金。
M|Right this way. The host will show you to a nice table with a white tablecloth.|這邊請。領檯會帶你們到有白色桌巾的好位子。
B|Thank you. May we have the menu and some water?|謝謝。可以給我們菜單和一些水嗎？
M|Of course. Would you like a cocktail from the bar, or some champagne in an ice bucket?|當然。你們想從吧檯點雞尾酒，還是冰桶香檳？
B|No alcohol, thanks. Tea in a teapot, please, and a coffee later.|不用酒，謝謝。請給我一壺茶，晚點再來咖啡。
M|The waitress will bring a coffee pot after dessert.|甜點後女服務生會拿咖啡壺來。
B|Please bring salt and pepper. I like fresh pepper from the pepper mill.|請拿鹽和胡椒來。我喜歡用研磨罐磨的新鮮胡椒。
M|Sure. Here are the napkins and a placemat for each of you.|好的。這是每位的餐巾和餐墊。
B|The service is great. Everyone is so polite.|服務真好，大家都很有禮貌。
M|Thank you. When you finish, pay at the counter, or I can bring the bill here.|謝謝。用完餐可以到櫃檯付款，或我把帳單拿來這裡。
B|Please bring the bill to the table. And don't forget the toothpicks!|請把帳單拿到桌上，也別忘了牙籤！
