B|Wow, Mom! Is this where the ambulance brings sick people?|哇，媽媽！救護車就是把病人載到這裡嗎？
M|Yes, that's the emergency room. And over there is the waiting room.|對，那是急診室。那邊是候診室。
B|What does the sign on this door say?|這扇門上的牌子寫什麼？
M|It says obstetrician. That doctor delivers babies. The next one is an ophthalmologist for the eyes.|寫著婦產科醫師，這位醫師接生嬰兒。下一間是看眼睛的眼科醫師。
B|And what do a pediatrician and an internist do?|那小兒科醫師和內科醫師做什麼？
M|A pediatrician treats children, and an internist treats adults for problems like stomachaches.|小兒科醫師治療小孩，內科醫師治療大人的問題，像是胃痛。
B|Do you work at the nurses' station?|你在護理站工作嗎？
M|Yes. I take care of each patient in the ward before and after surgery.|對。我在病房裡照顧每位病人，手術前和手術後都是。
B|Is that the operating room? Does a surgeon work there?|那是手術室嗎？外科醫師在那裡工作嗎？
M|Right. And if a patient is very sick, he stays in the ICU, the intensive care unit.|對。如果病人病得很重，就會住進加護病房。
B|There is also an ear, nose and throat doctor, and a dentist.|這裡還有耳鼻喉科醫師和牙醫。
M|Yes, a hospital has many kinds of doctors. I'm proud of my work!|對，醫院有很多種醫師。我為我的工作感到驕傲！
