--dialogue
B|Good morning, class. Please look at the blackboard.|各位同學早安，請看黑板。
M|Mr. Chen, is this our new class schedule?|陳老師，這是我們的新課表嗎？
B|Yes, it is. I'll write it on the blackboard, and you copy it.|對，我會寫在黑板上，你們把它抄下來。
M|I forgot my pencil box. Can I borrow a pencil?|我忘了帶鉛筆盒，我可以借一支鉛筆嗎？
B|Sure. Now, open your textbook to page five.|可以。現在請把課本翻到第五頁。
M|Is there a map in this book?|這本書裡有地圖嗎？
B|Yes, but we'll use the globe on my desk first. Come to the platform and find Japan.|有，不過我們先用我桌上的地球儀。上台來找找日本。
M|Here it is! Is the chalk on the desk?|在這裡！粉筆在桌上嗎？
B|Yes. Please erase the old words with the eraser, too.|是的。也請用板擦把舊的字擦掉。
M|Okay. Can you speak into the microphone? The back row can't hear.|好。你可以對著麥克風說話嗎？後排聽不到。
B|Of course. Listen, the public address system is on. The principal wants to say hello.|當然。聽，廣播系統開了，校長要跟大家問好。
M|What a good start to the new school year!|新學年有個好的開始！
