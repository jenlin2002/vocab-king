B|Kim, I just moved to Bigburg. Can you show me the city?|金，我剛搬到大堡市，你可以帶我看看這座城市嗎？

B|Where can I buy food?|哪裡可以買食物？
M|There is a bakery, a delicatessen, and a convenience store. A restaurant is also nearby.|有麵包店、熟食店和便利商店，附近也有餐廳。
B|What if I get sick?|如果我生病了呢？
M|The hospital is two blocks away, and the pharmacy is next to it.|醫院在兩個街區外，藥局就在它旁邊。
B|I need a fan and a sofa for my new apartment.|我新家需要一台電風扇和一張沙發。
M|Go to the appliance store and the furniture store. For a gift, try the toy store.|去家電行和家具行。要買禮物就去玩具店。
B|Is there a place for fun?|有好玩的地方嗎？
M|Yes, a movie theater, a gym, and a bookstore. The newsstand sells maps.|有電影院、健身房和書店，報攤有賣地圖。
B|And the hotel for my guests?|那我的客人要住的旅館呢？
M|Near the beauty salon. The police station and the fire hydrant are on the corner. Don't worry, you will love it here!|在美容院附近。警察局和消防栓在轉角。別擔心，你會喜歡這裡的！
