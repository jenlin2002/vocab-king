B|Class, our field trip begins at the harbor. Look at the ships!|同學們，我們的戶外教學從港口開始。看那些船！
M|Ms. Jane, is that island far from here?|珍老師，那座島離這裡遠嗎？
B|Yes, it is across the ocean. Today we visit a river, a forest, and a mountain.|對，在海的另一邊。今天我們要去看河、森林和山。
M|Is there a waterfall on the river?|河上有瀑布嗎？
B|Yes, and a dam farther down. Water from the dam gives us electricity.|有，下游還有一座水壩。水壩的水能發電。
M|What is the flat land next to the river?|河邊那片平坦的地是什麼？
B|That is a plain. Farmers grow rice there. A valley is between two mountains.|那是平原，農夫在那裡種稻。山谷在兩座山中間。
M|I see a pond and some woods over there!|我看到那邊有池塘和一些樹林！
B|Good eyes! The highest place on a mountain is the peak, and a plateau is high but flat.|你眼力真好！山上最高的地方是山峰，高原則是又高又平。
M|Is Taipei in a basin?|台北是在盆地裡嗎？
B|Yes. In the afternoon, we will go to a lake and a sandbar. Then we will have a picnic on the beach.|對。下午我們要去湖邊和沙洲，然後在海灘野餐。
M|I can't wait to see the sunset on the horizon!|我等不及要看地平線上的日落了！
