M|May, we are roommates now. Let's divide the housework.|梅，我們現在是室友了，來分配家事吧。

M|Fair. When you get up, sweep the floor and vacuum the carpet.|公平。你起床後，要掃地和吸地毯。

M|Do the laundry before you cook dinner. Wash the dishes right after we eat.|在煮晚餐前先洗衣服。吃完飯馬上洗碗。
B|You want me to cook, too? What are you going to do?|你也要我煮飯？那你要做什麼？
M|I'll watch TV, make phone calls, and read magazines.|我會看電視、打電話和看雜誌。
B|That's not fair! Turn off the TV and come in here.|這不公平！把電視關掉，過來這裡。
M|Okay, okay. I'll brush my teeth, wash my face, and then help you.|好啦好啦。我會刷牙、洗臉，然後幫你。
B|Good. Turn on the light and open the window. Let's clean together.|好。開燈、開窗，我們一起打掃。
