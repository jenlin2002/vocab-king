M|Ronald, you're a TV weather forecaster. How do you predict the weather?|羅納德，你是電視氣象主播，你怎麼預測天氣？

M|Why don't we ever get snow here?|為什麼我們這裡從不下雪？
B|The Pacific Ocean makes our weather mild. We rarely see frost or ice.|太平洋讓我們的天氣溫和，很少有霜或冰。
M|I remember a big storm once. A cold front blew in, and there was ice on the road.|我記得有一次大暴風雨。冷鋒吹進來，路上結冰。
B|Yes. We don't have typhoons or tornadoes here, so be happy.|對。我們這裡沒有颱風或龍捲風，所以要開心。
M|What was the worst storm you've ever seen?|你看過最糟的暴風雨是什麼？
B|The summer storm of 1998. There was thunder, lightning, and hail.|1998 年的夏季暴風雨。有雷聲、閃電和冰雹。
M|Did you predict it?|你有預測到嗎？
B|Yes! I said it would rain, with strong wind. I got a raise.|有！我說會下雨，還有強風。我因此加薪。
M|How did you predict it?|你怎麼預測到的？
B|Don't tell anyone, but it was a lucky guess. High pressure usually means sun, but fog and cloud are harder.|別告訴別人，那是幸運猜到的。高氣壓通常代表晴天，但霧和雲比較難預測。
