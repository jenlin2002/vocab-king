B|Dr. Wu, what is in the first-aid kit?|吳醫師，急救箱裡有什麼？
M|Many things. Here is a thermometer, an ear thermometer, and some face masks.|很多東西。這裡有溫度計、耳溫槍和一些口罩。
B|What do I use for a small cut?|小傷口要用什麼？
M|Clean it with a cotton ball, put on ointment, and cover it with gauze or a Band-Aid.|用棉球清潔、擦上藥膏，然後用紗布或 OK 繃蓋住。
B|And for a splinter?|如果有木刺呢？
M|Use tweezers. For a broken arm, we use a bandage and a sling.|用鑷子。如果手臂骨折，就用繃帶和三角吊帶。
B|What are tablets, pills, and capsules?|藥片、藥丸和膠囊是什麼？
M|They are different kinds of medicine you swallow. Always read the label.|它們是不同種類吞服的藥，一定要看標籤。
B|I saw a syringe at the clinic. Is it for drawing blood?|我在診所看到針筒，是用來抽血的嗎？
M|Yes, a nurse can draw blood with it. Sometimes a patient needs a blood transfusion.|對，護理師可以用它抽血。有時候病人需要輸血。
B|Can I use the stethoscope to hear my heart?|我可以用聽診器聽我的心跳嗎？
M|Of course. And I'll teach you CPR next. It can save a life!|當然可以。接下來我會教你心肺復甦術，它能救人一命！
