B|Tina, where do you want to go this summer?|蒂娜，你今年暑假想去哪裡？
M|I want to visit an island. Can we take a ferry?|我想去一座小島，我們可以搭渡輪嗎？
B|Yes, or we can go by sailboat or motorboat.|可以，或是坐帆船或摩托艇。
M|What about a long trip to America?|那去美國的長途旅行呢？
B|We would fly in a jumbo jet. Goods go in a container ship, and oil goes in a tanker.|我們會搭大型客機。貨物走貨櫃輪，石油走油輪。
M|I saw a submarine in a museum. It travels under the sea!|我在博物館看過潛水艇，它在海底航行！
B|A helicopter can land on a small place, and a biplane is an old kind of airplane.|直升機可以降落在很小的地方，雙翼飛機是一種老式飛機。
M|A hot air balloon looks so romantic!|熱氣球看起來好浪漫！
B|People also used a clipper to cross the ocean a long time ago.|很久以前人們也用快速帆船橫渡大洋。
M|And a raft is the simplest boat, right?|而木筏是最簡單的船，對嗎？
B|Right. Which one do you choose?|對。你選哪一種？
M|The ferry, Dad. Then we can enjoy the sea!|渡輪，爸爸。這樣我們就能欣賞大海了！
