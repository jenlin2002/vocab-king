--dialogue
B|Grace, what color do you want for the sky in your picture?|葛蕾絲，你的畫裡天空要用什麼顏色？
M|Blue, but a light blue. The ocean will be a dark blue.|藍色，不過是淺藍色。海洋會用深藍色。
B|I'll paint the sun yellow and the grass green.|我要把太陽畫成黃色、草地畫成綠色。
M|Can you mix red and yellow to make orange?|你可以把紅色和黃色混合變成橘色嗎？
B|Yes. And red and blue make purple.|可以。紅色加藍色就變成紫色。
M|I want pink flowers and a brown tree.|我想畫粉紅色的花和一棵棕色的樹。
B|What about the clouds? White or gray?|雲呢？白色還是灰色？
M|Gray clouds mean rain, so I'll use white. The sunset will be gold.|灰雲代表下雨，所以我用白色。夕陽用金色。
B|My robot is silver, and its eyes are black.|我的機器人是銀色的，眼睛是黑色的。
M|Wow, your picture looks so cool!|哇，你的畫看起來好酷！
B|Thanks. Yours is beautiful, too. Let's show them to the teacher.|謝謝，你的也很漂亮。我們拿給老師看吧。
M|Okay. I hope she likes the colors!|好。希望老師喜歡這些顏色！
