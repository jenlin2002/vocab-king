--dialogue
M|Paul, welcome to Bigburg! Let me show you the streets.|保羅，歡迎來到大堡市！我帶你看看這些街道。
B|Thanks, Kim. What is that big road?|謝謝你，金。那條大馬路是什麼？
M|That's the boulevard, with trees on both sides. The freeway is farther away.|那是林蔭大道，兩邊都有樹。高速公路在更遠的地方。
B|Where can I catch a bus?|我可以在哪裡搭公車？
M|At the bus stop on the corner. The subway entrance is across the street.|在街角的公車站。地鐵入口在對面。
B|How do I get there? There is a lot of traffic.|我怎麼過去？車很多。
M|Use the crosswalk at the intersection, but wait for the traffic light. Or use the pedestrian bridge.|在十字路口走斑馬線，但要等紅綠燈，或是走天橋。
B|Is there an underpass too?|還有地下道嗎？
M|Yes, near the gas station. Always walk on the sidewalk, away from the curb.|有，在加油站附近。要一直走在人行道上，離路緣遠一點。
B|It is dark at night. Are there streetlights?|晚上很暗，有路燈嗎？
M|Yes, and the street signs show every street name. Parking space is hard to find, though.|有，路牌上寫著每條街的名字。不過停車位很難找。
B|Thanks, Kim. I think I can find my way now!|謝謝你，金。我想我現在可以找到路了！
